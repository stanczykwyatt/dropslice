# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **One page for five formats:** NWB and HDF5, MATLAB v7.3 and v5, NumPy .npy and .npz, and Zarr v2 and v3 folders.
- **h5web's viewer** for all of them: line plots, heatmaps, RGB images, tables, and slicing through any dimension.
- **Big HDF5 files read lazily,** with compression plugins for Blosc, Zstandard, LZ4, LZF, bzip2 and more.
- **MATLAB v5** structs, cell arrays, text, logicals, complex numbers and sparse matrices, in MATLAB's dimensions.
- **Open public data by link** with `?url=`.
- **Nothing is uploaded,** with no cookies, storage or tracking, and pickled NumPy objects are never loaded.
